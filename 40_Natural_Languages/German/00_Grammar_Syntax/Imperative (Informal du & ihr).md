# Imperative (Informal du & ihr)

📝 **One-Sentence Summary**
The informal imperative gives commands to one person (`du`) by using the verb's stem, and to multiple people (`ihr`) by using the verb's standard present-tense `ihr`-form.

💡 **Core Rule & Analogy**
**The Rule:**
1.  **`du`-form:** Use the verb stem (infinitive minus `-en`). `(du gehst)` $\rightarrow$ `Geh!`
2.  **`ihr`-form:** Use the present tense `ihr`-form. `(ihr geht)` $\rightarrow$ `Geht!`

**Analogy:**
* The `ihr` command is a simple `call()` to the standard `ihr` verb form.
* The `du` command is a call to the "root" or "stem" function, which may have special conditions (like vowel changes).

⚙️ **Syntactical Structure**

| Pronoun | Verb (Present Tense) | Imperative Form |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **du** | `du gehst` | `Geh!` |
| **ihr** | `ihr geht` | `Geht!` |

**Important Exceptions for `du`-form:**
* **Vowel Change (e $\rightarrow$ i/ie):** If the `du` form changes the vowel, the imperative **keeps** that change.
    * `essen` $\rightarrow$ `du isst` $\rightarrow$ `Iss!`
    * `lesen` $\rightarrow$ `du liest` $\rightarrow$ `Lies!`
* **Vowel Change (a $\rightarrow$ ä):** If the `du` form adds an umlaut, the imperative does **not** take the umlaut.
    * `fahren` $\rightarrow$ `du fährst` $\rightarrow$ `Fahr!` (Not `Fähr!`)
* **Irregular `sein` (to be):**
    * `du bist` $\rightarrow$ `Sei!`
    * `ihr seid` $\rightarrow$ `Seid!`

🚀 **Examples in Context**
* **du (regular):** `Trink jetzt einen Kaffee!` (Drink a coffee now!)
* **du (vowel change):** `Iss etwas Gesundes!` (Eat something healthy!)
* **du (irregular):** `Sei doch nicht faul!` (Don't be lazy!)
* **ihr (regular):** `Kommt doch mit!` (Come with!)
* **ihr (irregular):** `Seid viel draußen!` (Be outside a lot!)
