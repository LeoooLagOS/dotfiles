# Modal Verbs

## 📝 One-Sentence Summary
Modal verbs modify the mood of an action (necessity, ability, permission) and push the main action verb to the end of the sentence in its infinitive form.

## 💡 Core Rule & Analogy
**The "Sentence Bracket" (Satzklammer):**
Imagine the sentence is a bracket.
* The **Modal Verb** opens the bracket at Position 2.
* The **Infinitive** (Main Verb) closes the bracket at the very end.
* Everything else (Time, Manner, Place) is stuffed inside the bracket.

## ⚙️ Syntactical Structure
**Present Tense:**
`Subject + [Modal Verb] + ... + [Infinitive]`

**The 6 Modals:**
1.  **können** (ability/can)
2.  **müssen** (necessity/must)
3.  **wollen** (intent/want)
4.  **dürfen** (permission/may)
5.  **sollen** (duty/should)
6.  **mögen** (preference/like)

## 🚀 Examples in Context
**Standard Bracket:**
* *Ich **kann** heute nicht **kommen**.* (I cannot come today.)
* *Wir **müssen** für die Prüfung **lernen**.* (We must study for the exam.)

**The Präteritum Exception:**
While spoken German usually uses *Perfekt* for the past, Modal Verbs almost always use **Präteritum**.
* *Ich **musste** gestern arbeiten.* (I had to work yesterday.)
* *Er **wollte** das nicht essen.* (He didn't want to eat that.)
