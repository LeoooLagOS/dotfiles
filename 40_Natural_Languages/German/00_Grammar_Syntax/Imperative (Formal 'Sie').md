# Imperative (Formal 'Sie')

📝 **One-Sentence Summary**
The formal imperative is used to give commands, instructions, or polite advice to people you address with 'Sie,' and it's formed by using the infinitive verb followed by 'Sie'.

💡 **Core Rule & Analogy**
**The Rule:** To form the formal imperative, place the infinitive form of the verb at the beginning of the sentence, followed immediately by the pronoun 'Sie'.

**Analogy:** Think of it as a **direct command or function call**. The verb is the function name, and 'Sie' is the mandatory parameter specifying who should execute the command: `essen(Sie, Gemüse)!`

⚙️ **Syntactical Structure**
The sentence structure inverts the standard statement form, placing the verb first.

| Statement (Aussage) | Command (Imperativ) |
| :--- | :--- |
| `Sie machen Sport.` | `Machen Sie Sport!` |
| `Sie essen Gemüse.` | `Essen Sie Gemüse!` |
| `Sie gehen nach draußen.` | `Gehen Sie nach draußen!` |

**Irregular Verbs:** A few verbs are irregular. The most important is `sein`:
- `Sie sind ruhig.` → `**Seien Sie** ruhig!`

🚀 **Examples in Context**
- **Essen Sie** Vollkornprodukte, viel Obst und Gemüse! (Eat whole-grain products, lots of fruit and vegetables!)
- **Machen Sie** oft Sport! (Do sports often!)
- **Gehen Sie** täglich nach draußen! (Go outside daily!)
- **Fahren Sie** nicht immer mit dem Auto.  (Don't always go by car.)

🔗 **Connections**
- This is the formal version of giving commands. The informal counterparts are the [[Imperative (Informal 'du')]] and [[Imperative (Informal 'ihr')]].
- This grammatical mood is essential for giving advice, following instructions, or making polite requests.
