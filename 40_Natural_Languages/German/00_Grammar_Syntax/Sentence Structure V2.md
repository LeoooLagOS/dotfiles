# Main Clause Structure (The V2 Rule)

## 📝 One-Sentence Summary
In a main German clause, the conjugated verb **must** always be the second element, regardless of what comes first.

## 💡 Core Rule & Analogy
**Analogy: The King's Throne**
The Verb is the King. The King sits on the Throne (Position 2). The King never moves. The Subject (Knight) and Time (Jester) can swap places around him, but the King stays put.

## ⚙️ Syntactical Structure
$$Position 1 (Topic) + Position 2 (Verb) + Position 3 (Subject/Other)$$

### The "Inversion" Hack
To make your German sound better, start with **Time** or **Place**.
* *Standard:* **Ich** (1) **gehe** (2) heute ins Kino.
* *Better:* **Heute** (1) **gehe** (2) **ich** (3) ins Kino.
* *Wrong:* ~~Heute ich gehe ins Kino.~~ (The King is pushed to position 3 = Illegal).

## 🚀 Examples in Context
1.  **Subject Start:** Der Hund **beißt** den Mann.
2.  **Object Start:** Den Mann **beißt** der Hund. (Emphasis on the man being bitten).
3.  **Time Start:** Morgen **habe** ich keine Zeit.
