# The Perfekt Tense (Spoken Past)

## 📝 One-Sentence Summary
The primary tense used for spoken past events, constructed using a "Helper Verb" (Position 2) and a "Participle" (End Position).

## 💡 Core Rule & Analogy
**Analogy: The Sentence Bracket (The Sandwich)**
* **Top Bun (Position 2):** The Helper Verb (*haben* or *sein*).
* **Meat/Contents:** Time, Place, Objects.
* **Bottom Bun (End):** The Past Participle (*ge-*-word).
* *Rule:* You cannot eat the sandwich without both buns.

## ⚙️ Syntactical Structure
$$Subject + Helper (haben/sein) + ... Middle Field ... + Participle$$

### 1. The Helper Choice
* **Sein (to be):** Use for **Movement** (A to B) or **Change of State** (waking up, dying, melting).
    * *Examples:* gehen, fahren, fliegen, aufstehen.
* **Haben (to have):** Use for everything else (Stationary / Transitive).
    * *Examples:* essen, schlafen, arbeiten, kaufen.

### 2. The Participle (Partizip II)
* **Regular:** `ge` + stem + `t` (e.g., *machen* $\rightarrow$ *gemacht*)
* **Irregular:** `ge` + stem + `en` (e.g., *fahren* $\rightarrow$ *gefahren*)

## 🚀 Examples in Context
* **Movement (Sein):** Ich **bin** nach Berlin **gefahren**.
* **Stationary (Haben):** Ich **habe** eine Pizza **gegessen**.
* **Sentence Bracket:** Er **hat** gestern Abend lange **gearbeitet**.
