# Gym Vocabulary (Fitness und Training)

## 📝 One-Sentence Summary
Essential terminology for the "Bulking Phase," covering equipment, anatomy, and common gym interactions.

## 💡 Memory Hook
* **The "Machine" Gender:** Many mechanical devices in German are male (*Der*), but general concepts are often female (*Die*).
    * *Der* Satz (The set), *Der* Plan (The plan).
    * *Die* Übung (The exercise), *Die* Pause (The break).

## 🚀 Phrases in Context
1.  **Availability:** *"Ist das hier noch frei?"* (Is this still free?)
2.  **Sets:** *"Wie viele Sätze hast du noch?"* (How many sets do you have left?)
3.  **Status:** *"Ich bin fertig."* (I am finished.)
