# Housing Furniture (Wohnen und Möbel)

## 📝 One-Sentence Summary
Core vocabulary for describing living spaces, essential for navigating the "Dative Location" rules (e.g., *Der Tisch* $\rightarrow$ *Am Tisch*).

## 💡 Memory Hook (Gender)
* **Rule of Thumb:** Most rooms ending in `-e` are **Die** (Die Küche), but `-zimmer` is always **Das** (Das Wohnzimmer).

## 🚀 Phrases in Context
* **Die Lage:** Ich wohne **auf dem Land** (in the countryside) vs. **in der Stadt** (in the city).
* **Location:** Das Kissen liegt **auf dem** Bett.
