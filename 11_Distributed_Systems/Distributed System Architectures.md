# Distributed System Architectures

## 📝 One-Sentence Summary
The logical and physical organization of software components and their interactions within a distributed network.

## 💡 Analogy
**The Restaurant Chain:** 
- **Centralized:** One kitchen serves the whole city (Single point of failure).
- **Client-Server:** A main kitchen (Server) sends food to various satellite dining rooms (Clients).
- **Decentralized/P2P:** Every home is a kitchen, and neighbors swap ingredients directly.

## 🔑 Key Details
- **Logical Architecture:** How components are structured (e.g., [[Layered]], [[Object-based]], [[Data-centered]]).
- **System Architectures:**
    - **Centralized:** Traditional Client-Server model.
    - **Decentralized:** Peer-to-Peer (P2P) networks.
    - **Hybrid:** Combining both (e.g., Edge Computing).
