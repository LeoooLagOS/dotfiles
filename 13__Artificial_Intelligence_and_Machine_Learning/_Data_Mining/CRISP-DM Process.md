# CRISP-DM Process

## 📝 One-Sentence Summary
The Cross-Industry Standard Process for Data Mining (CRISP-DM) is a structured methodology for planning and executing a data mining project.

## 💡 Analogy
It is the "[[SDLC|Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC)]]" but specifically for data; you wouldn't build a house without a blueprint, and you don't mine data without CRISP-DM.

## 🔑 Key Details
- **What it is:** A non-linear, 6-phase iterative process.
- **Why it exists:** To ensure the data analysis aligns with business goals and is reproducible.
- **The 6 Phases:**
	1. **Business Understanding:** What is the goal?
	2. **Data Understanding:** What data do we have?
	3. **Data Preparation:** Cleaning and formatting (The most time-consuming step).
	4. **Modeling:** Applying the algorithms.
	5. **Evaluation:** Does the model actually solve the business goal?
	6. **Deployment:** Putting the model into a production environment.

## 🔗 Connections & Implementations
- **Implementation:** Used in every major Data Science project to prevent "Scope Creep."
- **Connection:** Directly feeds into [[Machine Learning Pipelines]].

## 👍 Pros & 👎 Cons
- **Pros:** Robust, industry-standard, promotes documentation.
- **Cons:** Can feel slow for small, agile exploratory projects.
