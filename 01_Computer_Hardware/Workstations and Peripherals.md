# Workstations and Peripherals

## 📝 One-Sentence Summary
The physical hardware endpoints (Workstations) and the auxiliary devices (Peripherals) that extend a computer's capabilities within a networked environment.

## 💡 Analogy
In a cockpit, the **Workstation** is the pilot's seat and control panel where the main processing happens; the **Peripherals** are the sensors, radio, and landing gear—tools that allow the pilot to interact with the outside world.

## 🔑 Key Details
- **What it is:** The hardware-level interface of a system.
- **Workstations:** High-performance standalone computers designed for technical or scientific applications.
- **Peripherals:** Any device that connects to the workstation (Input: Keyboard/Mouse; Output: Monitor/Printer; Storage: External Drives).

## 🔗 Connections & Implementations
- **Physical Layer:** Related to [[RS232]] and [[_Networking Devices]].
- **Administration:** Managed via device [[Drivers]] and [[Kernel Modules]] in [[_Operating Systems]].

## 👍 Pros & 👎 Cons
- **Pros:** Modular expansion of system capabilities.
- **Cons:** Increases the "Surface Area" for hardware failure and driver conflicts.
