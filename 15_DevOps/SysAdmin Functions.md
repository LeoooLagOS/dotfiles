# SysAdmin Functions

## 📝 One-Sentence Summary
The foundational practices of managing system infrastructure, ensuring uptime, security, and resource allocation for multi-user environments.

## 💡 Analogy
The **Pit Crew** in a Formula 1 race. They don't drive the car (User), but they ensure the engine is tuned, the tires are replaced (Maintenance), and the fuel is managed so the car can finish the race safely.

## 🔑 Key Details
- **What it is:** The bridge between hardware management and software reliability.
- **Why it exists:** To maintain the "Health" of the system through the entire lifecycle.

## ⚙️ Core Components
- **User Management:** Provisioning access and managing [[SSH]] keys.
- **Security:** Hardening the system against vulnerabilities ([[Firewall]]s, Updates).
- **Automation:** The transition point into Modern [[_DevOps]] (Scripting repetitive tasks).

## 🔗 Connections & Implementations
- **Prerequisite:** Foundation for [[SRE|SRE (Site Reliability Engineering)]].
## 👍 Pros & 👎 Cons
- **Pros:** Ensures high availability and data integrity.
- **Cons:** Often reactive; modern DevOps aims to move these functions to a proactive/automated state.
