# _macOS_

## 📝 One-Sentence Summary
macOS is a series of proprietary graphical [[_Operating Systems]]  developed and marketed by Apple Inc., known for its strong focus on user experience, design, and its [[Unix]]-based foundation.
## 💡 Analogy
macOS is like a **high-end, luxury car from a single manufacturer**. The hardware (the Mac) and the software (macOS) are designed by the same company to work together perfectly, creating a seamless, highly polished, and integrated user experience. You can't install the engine (macOS) in another company's car body (non-Apple hardware).
## 📜 History & Creator
Modern macOS has its roots in **NeXTSTEP**, an operating system developed by NeXT, a company founded by **Steve Jobs** after he left Apple in the 1980s. When Apple acquired NeXT in 1997, NeXTSTEP became the foundation for Mac OS X, first released in 2001. 
This replaced the "Classic" Mac OS and brought a powerful, stable, Unix-based core to Apple's computers for the first time.
## 🔑 Core Philosophy & Design 
The core philosophy of macOS is the **tight integration of hardware and software**. 
- **Unix-Certified:** 
	- The core of macOS is a certified **[[Unix]]** operating system (based on the BSD family), which provides a powerful and stable foundation. 
- **User Experience First:** 
	- The primary design goal is to create a consistent, intuitive, and aesthetically pleasing graphical user interface. 
- **"It Just Works":** 
	- Apple controls the entire ecosystem (hardware and software), which allows them to ensure a high degree of reliability and seamless interoperability between their devices.
## ⚙️ Key Details 
- **What it is:** 
	- The proprietary operating system for Apple's line of Macintosh computers. 
- **Why it exists:** 
	- To provide a highly polished, user-friendly, and stable operating system designed to work perfectly with Apple's own hardware. 
- **Key Components:** 
	- **Darwin:** The open-source, Unix-like core of the OS, which includes the **[[XNU Kernel]]**. 
	- **Aqua:** The proprietary graphical user interface. 
	- **Cocoa and Carbon:** The primary application programming interfaces (APIs) for developing native macOS applications.
## 🔗 Connections & Implementations
- **Command Line:** Because of its Unix core, macOS has a powerful, native command-line environment (using `zsh` as the default shell) that is very similar to [[_Linux|Linux]].
