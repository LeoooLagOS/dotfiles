# Vault Structure Philosophy

This document explains the logic behind the numbered folder structure of my Personal Knowledge Base. The goal is to create a scalable, non-alphabetical system that groups related topics into broad categories based on numerical ranges.

---
### ## 00-19: Core Knowledge, Theory & Reference 📚
This range is for the foundational, theoretical pillars of Computer Science and core technical domains. These are the timeless, universal principles and essential areas of study. The order within this range attempts to follow a logical progression from fundamental concepts to more complex systems.
- **`00_CS_Fundamentals/`**
- **`01_Computer_Hardware/`**
- **`02_Computer_Architecture/`**
- **`03_Operating_Systems/`**
- **`04_Concurrency_and_Parallelism/`**
- **`05_Algorithms_and_Data_Structures/`**
- **`06_Networking/`**
- **`07_Cybersecurity/`**
- **`08_Git/`**
- **`09_DevOps/`**

---
### ## 20-29: Applied Skills & Tools 🛠️
This range is for the practical tools (languages, frameworks) and the application of core knowledge within specific domains (like web or mobile development).
- **`20_Languages/`** 
- **`21_Web_Development/`**

---
### ## 30-39: Portfolio & Practice 🚀
This is for my hands-on work, personal projects, and coding practice where I synthesize and apply knowledge from the other sections.
- **`30_Projects/`** 
---
### ## 40-49: Personal Growth & Setup 🌱🖥️
This area is for knowledge outside of my core CS studies (like natural languages) or specific documentation related to my personal machine configurations.
- **`40_Natural_Languages/`**
- **`41_My_Setup/`** 

---
### ## 80-99: System & Meta ⚙️
This range is reserved for utility and system-related folders concerning the knowledge base itself. High numbers keep them at the bottom of the file list.
- **`80_System/`**
- **`90_Templates/`**
