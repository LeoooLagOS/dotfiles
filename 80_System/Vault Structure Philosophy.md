# 🏛️ Vault Structure Philosophy

This document outlines the logic and architectural principles of this Personal Knowledge Base (PKB). The system uses a **Numerical Range Hierarchy** rather than alphabetical sorting to ensure that related concepts remain grouped together as the vault scales.

---

## 00-19: Core Knowledge & Theory 📚
This range contains the foundational, theoretical pillars of Computer Science. These are the timeless principles that form the basis of all technical work. The progression moves from low-level hardware to high-level system abstractions.

- **00-02: Fundamentals & Hardware** (CS Theory, Logic, Digital Hardware, Architecture)
- **03-04: System Logic** (Operating Systems, Concurrency, Parallelism)
- **05-08: Software Foundations** (Algorithms, Data Structures, Graphics, Databases)
- **09-11: Infrastructure** (Networking, Cybersecurity, Distributed Systems)
- **13-15: Advanced Operations** (AI/ML, Git, DevOps/SysAdmin)



---

## 20-29: Applied Skills & Tools 🛠️
This range covers the practical application of core theories. It is dedicated to specific technologies, frameworks, and specialized development domains that change more frequently than core theory.

- **20_Languages:** Syntax and logic for specific programming languages (C, Python, Java).
- **21_Web_Development:** Frontend and Backend architecture.
- **22_Mobile_Development:** Android development and Event-Driven paradigms.

---

## 30-39: Portfolio & Practice 🚀
The "Output" zone. This area is for hands-on application, university laboratories, and personal projects where theoretical knowledge from the 00-29 ranges is synthesized into functional work.

- **30_Projects:** Active and archived development projects.
- **31_Uni_Labs:** Specific course assignments and practical reports.

---

## 40-49: Personal Growth & Setup 🌱
Knowledge and documentation that falls outside the core Computer Science curriculum or pertains to the specific configuration of the physical workstation.

- **40_Natural_Languages:** Language acquisition logs (e.g., German B1-B2 Journey).
- **41_My_Setup:** Documentation for the Fedora Linux environment, Hyprland configs, and dotfiles.

---

## 80-99: System, Logs & Meta ⚙️
The "Engine Room" of the vault. This range is dedicated to the maintenance, automation, and real-time monitoring of the system and personal performance.

- **80_System:** Vault philosophy, health check scripts, and architectural maintenance.
- **90_Logs (Log_DB):** Dynamic, temporal data. This includes career tracking (Job Hunt), physical health monitoring (Lumbar Tracker), and daily study logs.
- **91_Templates:** Standardized Blueprints for creating new notes, classes, or project entries to ensure structural consistency.



---

## System Mechanics

1. **Numerical Priority:** All top-level folders must start with a two-digit prefix to maintain a fixed order.
2. **Atomic Notes:** Concepts should be broken down into the smallest possible functional units.
3. **Temporal Logging:** While folders 00-49 house "Static Knowledge," folder 90 houses "Dynamic Events," allowing the vault to act as both an encyclopedia and a history log.
