# DDoS (Distributed Denial of Service)

## 📝 One-Sentence Summary
*A DDoS attack is a coordinated attempt to make an online service unavailable by overwhelming it with traffic from multiple compromised devices (zombies).*
## 💡 Analogy
*Imagine trying to get through a single doorway (the server) when a massive, coordinated crowd (zombie computers) suddenly rushes it from all directions, blocking anyone legitimate (real users) from getting in or out.*
## 🔑 Key Details
- **What it is:** 
	- A denial-of-service attack originating from numerous sources, often a botnet of infected computers. 
- **Why it exists:** 
	- The goal is to degrade or completely halt public access to an organization's resources, such as websites or online applications, by exhausting network bandwidth or server processing capacity.
## 🔗 Connections & Implementations
* DDoS attacks are a scaled-up version of a simpler [[DoS (Denial of Service)]] attack. 
* Mitigation often involves specialized DDoS protection services, [[Firewall|Firewalls]], and Intrusion Prevention Systems ([[IPS (Intrustion Prevention System)]]).
